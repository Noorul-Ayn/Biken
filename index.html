Sound familiar?

Getting a moto shouldn't feel like a negotiation.

For many foreigners in Kigali, a quick ride can mean a language barrier, haggling over an unclear fare and sometimes ending up in the wrong place. Biken takes all of that off your hands.

Without Biken
  • Trying to explain where you're going in a language you don't speak
  • Haggling over the fare, and often paying a "foreigner price"
  • Getting dropped off at the wrong place
  • Working it all out alone at the roadside
With Biken
  • Tell us in English, French or Kiswahili, and ride with someone who speaks it too
  • A standard fare we agree with the rider before you ride
  • Your pickup and destination confirmed with the rider in advance
  • A real Biken coordinator handling the details for you
